# PantryPilot User Personas

## Persona 1: Busy Family Planner
- Name: Sarah (38)
- Household: Two working parents, two kids
- Goal: Plan a full week of meals in under 20 minutes
- Pain Points:
  - Last-minute "what's for dinner" stress
  - Forgotten ingredients and duplicate buying
  - Limited time for complex prep
- Key Needs from PantryPilot:
  - Fast weekly meal planner
  - Family-friendly recipe filters
  - Shared visibility of pantry status

## Persona 2: Meal-Prep Optimizer
- Name: Daniel (31)
- Household: Lives with partner, cooks in batches
- Goal: Use ingredients efficiently across multiple meals
- Pain Points:
  - Overbuying produce and proteins
  - Hard to track what should be cooked first
  - Repetitive planning overhead each week
- Key Needs from PantryPilot:
  - Inventory-linked recipe recommendations
  - Expiry-aware planning prompts
  - Reusable weekly prep templates

## Persona 3: Beginner Confidence Cook
- Name: Aisha (24)
- Household: Solo renter, new to cooking
- Goal: Cook simple meals confidently without confusion
- Pain Points:
  - Uncertainty about cooking sequence and timing
  - Difficulty choosing recipes matching skill level
  - Waste from failed attempts and spoiled ingredients
- Key Needs from PantryPilot:
  - Step-by-step guided cooking mode
  - Difficulty-labeled recipes with time estimates
  - Ingredient substitutions and recovery tips

## Shared Persona Insights
- Time-to-value must be immediate in onboarding
- Inventory entry must stay lightweight to avoid drop-off
- Guidance and recommendations should adapt to skill and routine
